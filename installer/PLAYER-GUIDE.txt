MY FURRY PROTOGEN | VN REVIVAL
Windows x64 · Steam 11915262 · Installer 1.0.0

Extract this ZIP and keep its files together. Close the game, open
Protogen Localization Installer.exe, then click Install localization.
If Steam discovery fails, choose My Furry Protogen under steamapps/common.

Click Launch game. Choose among 30 translations in Preferences > Language.
The installer detects the base game or the official 18+ DLC from the installed
scripts. Run it again after adding or removing the DLC.

Use Launch game in the installer for the localized Ren'Py 8.2.3 runtime.
Steam's Play button starts the original Ren'Py 8.0.3 runtime, which lacks
the shaping needed by Arabic, Persian, Hindi and Thai. The two launch paths
use the game's existing save locations. Close the game before changing the
installed package. Keep the extracted installer for future launches.
